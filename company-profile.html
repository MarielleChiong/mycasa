Our Story
Built on a Foundation of Thirty Years

Founded on the core principles of family, integrity, and technical mastery, MYCASA Construction Services stands as the vital bridge between decades of veteran experience and modern engineering precision.

Our technical heritage was established in 1996 under the leadership of Marcelo Chiong, Senior Technical Consultant and Electrical Engineer, who brings 30 years of field-proven expertise to every project we undertake. Today, MYCASA is managed by a new generation of siblings — dedicated to upholding Mr. Chiong's legacy of quality while meeting the advanced standards and expectations that modern clients demand.

Under the direct technical guidance of Marcelo Chiong, we leverage three decades of specialized experience in Technical Project Supervision. Having led and coordinated multi-trade teams for 30 years, Mr. Chiong ensures that every stage of work — from electrical systems to final finishing — is executed exactly according to specifications, with the precision that defines our reputation.

Fun Fact: The name MYCASA is more than just a brand — it is a family acronym woven from the names of Engr. Marcelo Chiong's six children: Marielle Honey (eldest), Young Moses (youngest), Kizzia Charm (4th), April Pearl (2nd), Sweet Chaste (5th), and Ammon Gabriel (3rd, eldest son). Every letter carries a name, and every project carries the family's promise.

Experience & Heritage
Three Decades of Project Mastery

While MYCASA represents a modern, forward-moving firm, our leadership and technical team have been a constant, trusted presence in the construction industry for 30 years. Since 1996, under the technical direction of Marcelo Chiong, our work has been deeply rooted in serving The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints — acting as a preferred contractor for their facilities across the region.

Projects Successfully Delivered:

  • Chapels and Meetinghouses — new construction, expansion, and full interior/exterior renovation
  • Seminary and Institute Buildings — structural upgrades, system installations, and finishing works
  • Administrative & Office Facilities — fit-outs, maintenance, and compliance upgrades
  • Auxiliary Properties & Groundworks — structural repairs, utility upgrades, and long-term preservation

Our long-standing partnership is built on understanding the unique requirements, standards, and expectations that come with sacred and institutional spaces. We have developed deep expertise in working within strict guidelines, preserving design integrity, and delivering work that reflects respect, quality, and durability. Every project carries the same care and precision established in our very first assignment for the Church in 1996 — a standard we bring to every client today.
