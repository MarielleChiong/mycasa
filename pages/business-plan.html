Executive Summary

MYCASA Construction Services is a newly launched family construction firm based in Cebu, Philippines, founded on core values of family, integrity, precision, mentorship, and accountability. Technical leadership is provided by Marcelo Chiong, a licensed Electrical Engineer with 30 years of proven expertise in project supervision and specialized electrical works. The company is managed by Marielle Honey Chiong as Project Manager and Owner, bringing modern business management and safety expertise to uphold the family legacy.

Our services include specialized electrical works, architectural refinishing, surface & masonry restoration, and minor structural repairs & maintenance. The family carries a long trusted track record serving institutions like The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints for decades. MYCASA continues this legacy while expanding to serve more clients across residential, commercial, and institutional sectors.

Current Status: All business licenses and legal documents are complete. We are operating from a home base while final regulatory requirements are being completed as we grow.
